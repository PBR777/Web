Concave vs. Convex Lab

  • • Diverging Nature (Concave): Switch to Concave mode with a Parallel Comb beam. Notice how rays bend outward upon refraction. Their virtual dashed extensions map directly back to focal point F'!
  • • Virtual Focus Alignment: Point a single ray towards virtual focus F on the right of a concave lens. The refracted ray emerges perfectly parallel to the principal axis.
